01 / LOCALFISCAL

Keep a small ledger local.

Fictional amounts in a fresh scratch database.

$ localfiscal add 2026-10-19 "Example Client" 1200.00 --income
ADDED: 1 | $1,200.00 | income

$ localfiscal add 2026-10-19 "Example Supplies" 18.49 --category office
ADDED: 2 | $18.49 | expense

02 / LOCALFISCAL

Read the generated report.

The real report file, with integer minor units.

$ localfiscal report --fmt json
